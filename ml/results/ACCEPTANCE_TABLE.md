**Q6 is defined on the test alert rate, with a limit of 25%** ("The alert rate at the chosen cutoff is at most **25%** of agents", marked *(test)*). The test alert rate is 16.7%, so Q6 passes. The 15% figure is the Step 7 and 8 alert budget (`metrics.ALERT_BUDGET`), used only to compare baselines and default models at a common operating point before the cutoff was chosen. It is not an acceptance criterion. The test alert rate (16.7%) is above that 15% comparison budget and above the validation alert rate (13.3%); this is reported, not acted on.

**Q1 note:** the criterion text names the v3a value (0.541). On the change 5 data the Step 7 best single-feature validation PR AUC is 0.574. Q1 passes against both.
