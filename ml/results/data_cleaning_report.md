## Decision: failed and pending activations
Activation status mix: completed 85.0%, pending 9.9%, failed 5.1%. **Failed and pending records count toward submission_frequency.** The metric measures submission behaviour (attempts), fake customers are the submissions most likely to fail, and the live backend pipeline counts every record. In this synthetic data status is independent of fraud, so the choice has no effect here and cannot be validated on it.
